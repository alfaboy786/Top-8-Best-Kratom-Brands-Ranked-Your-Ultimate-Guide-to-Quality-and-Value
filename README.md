*   **Red Vein:** Known for relaxation and comfort. Best for evening use.

*   **Green Vein:** A hybrid balance of the two. Best for social situations or midday boosts.Brands like **Kratom Crazy** offer variety packs that allow you to test all three to see what works for your body chemistry.
